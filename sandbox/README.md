# The sandbox

To try your own email, save it as an .eml file (most mail apps can download or save a message that way) and put it in `inbox/` with a name that sorts after the demo's, such as `14-yourname.eml`. Address it to the owner, `whizz@fastmail.com`, alone in To and with nobody in Cc, sent from your own address: that is how a request forwarded from the owner's mailbox reaches her. Give it its own Message-ID and a Date on Tue, 01 Dec 2026 between the demo's last email (3:06 PM Pacific) and the 5:00 PM digest, and add a line for it to `inbox/facts.json`: `"14-yourname.eml": {"forwarded": "yes"}`. Then run the demo again: it starts the day fresh (the last run is kept in `state/old/`), and your email arrives with the demo's. With no `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` set there is no recorded model answer for your email, so she holds it, sends nothing, and says which key to add.

`outbox/` is her Sent folder, empty in a fresh clone. After a run it holds one .eml for every message she sent, named `<n>-<template>.eml`: each reply goes from her address to the sender, with a blind copy to the owner that is recorded in `outbox/envelope.jsonl` and never shown in a header (a booking or a cancel carries its .ics file), and her hold notices and the digest go to the owner. Any mail app opens them. `outbox/keywords.json` records how she filed each email she read, because she never moves or deletes mail, and her bookings appear in `calendar/` as `doris-*.ics` beside the owner's busy blocks.
